# Quick Email Setup - 2 Minutes!

## Easiest Method: FormSubmit (No Account Needed!)

1. **Open `index.html`**
2. **Find line 287** (the `<form>` tag)
3. **Replace `YOUR_EMAIL@example.com`** with your actual email address

For example, if your email is `dieum@gmail.com`, change:
```html
<form class="contact-form" id="contactForm" action="https://formsubmit.co/YOUR_EMAIL@example.com" method="POST">
```

To:
```html
<form class="contact-form" id="contactForm" action="https://formsubmit.co/dieum@gmail.com" method="POST">
```

4. **Save the file**
5. **Refresh your browser**
6. **Test the form** - you'll receive emails!

That's it! FormSubmit is free and works immediately.

---

## What Email Address Should You Use?

Use any email address you have access to:
- Gmail: `yourname@gmail.com`
- Outlook: `yourname@outlook.com`
- Any other email provider

**Important:** After the first submission, FormSubmit will send you a confirmation email. Click the confirmation link to start receiving messages.
